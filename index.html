RB–03 / Operator manual

Field guide

Three unlicensed signals share this receiver. Power on to hear the current station.

  • 88.3 · Cassini DriftDub chords and sub-bass bouncing between the rings.
  • 96.7 · Ice ChoirA slow bell choir broadcasting from the plumes of Enceladus.
  • 105.1 · Hex StaticStaccato electro from inside the north polar storm.

Drag the dial sideways or up and down. Tap it to scan, or select a preset. Use ← / → to tune, 1–3 for presets, Space for power, and M to mute.

When Aircheck is available, choose Record 12 s while on air. Tune between stations, then choose Stop clip or let the timer finish. Choose Play clip to listen in the receiver, or Save clip to download it.
